## 🧰 About the project

**Gliora** is a portal of free tools (personal finance, investing, productivity, utilities, health and more) optimized for **organic SEO**. It combines interactive calculators and utilities with a large-scale, generated encyclopedia of concepts.

## ✨ What it offers

- 🧮 ~150 interactive **tools** across 12 categories.
- 📚 A custom **Knowledge Graph** that generates thousands of interlinked content pages (~3,550 pages in total).
- 🔢 **Executable formula sheets** with native MathML and code snippets.
- ⚡ A relentless focus on **performance** (Core Web Vitals) and SEO.

## 🛠️ Tech stack

- **Astro** (SSG) + **TypeScript** + **SCSS**, with a centralized, token-based design system.
- **Zero runtime dependencies** as a general rule (minimal bundle); only a few exceptions, lazily loaded.
- A custom **Knowledge Graph generator** (curated seeds + automatic generation) as the only way to sustain the scale.

## 👤 My role

End-to-end design and development: site architecture, the Knowledge Graph engine, the tools system and the technical SEO and performance strategy.
